About

I am a physicist and space systems engineer, working as a Subsystem Engineer at the German Aerospace Center (DLR). I hold an MSc in Space Science from the International Space University, specializing in Space Engineering & Applications, and a BSc in Physics, specializing in electronics, from the University of Patras.

My expertise covers CubeSats, space robotics and electrical power systems, from power budgeting and PCB design to integration, verification and testing. For my Master's thesis, I designed a custom power management and distribution PCB for a 1U CubeSat, combining solar MPPT input, battery protection and orbital energy simulation. I enjoy working in international, multidisciplinary teams.
